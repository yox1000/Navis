# Floor Plan Images Required

Place the following floor plan images in this directory:

## library_f1.png
- Dimensions: 1000x700 pixels
- Shows ground floor layout of library
- Key landmarks at coordinates from library_demo.json:
  - Entrance at bottom center (500, 650)
  - Circulation desk at left (300, 400)  
  - Elevator at right side (800, 300)
  - Corridors connecting areas

## library_f2.png  
- Dimensions: 1000x700 pixels
- Shows second floor layout
- Key landmarks:
  - Elevator at right side (800, 300)
  - Study areas in center (500, 400)
  - Reference section at top right (600, 200)
  - Corridors connecting areas

## Notes for Floor Plan Creation
- Use simple line drawings or architectural sketches
- Mark elevator and staircase locations clearly
- Include basic room outlines
- Use consistent scale matching coordinate system
- Save as PNG format for transparency support
- Keep file sizes reasonable (< 500KB each)

## Temporary Workaround
For testing, you can use any 1000x700 PNG images as placeholders. The app will overlay navigation paths on top of these background images.
